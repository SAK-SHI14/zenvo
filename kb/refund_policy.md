# Zenvo Refund and Cancellation Policy

We want every clinic to feel confident trying Zenvo.

## Monthly Plans
- You can cancel your subscription anytime before your next billing date with zero cancellation fees.
- When you cancel, your plan remains active until the end of the current paid billing cycle.

## 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee
- First-time paid subscribers on Basic and Pro plans are eligible for a full 100% refund within 14 days of their first payment if they are not satisfied.
- To request a refund, email billing@zenvo.in with your clinic name and registered phone number.
- Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Annual & Enterprise Plans
- Annual plans refunded within 14 days receive a full refund; after 14 days, refunds are not issued for unused months, though you may pause your account for up to 60 days.
- Enterprise custom contracts follow the refund terms specified in their signed agreement.
