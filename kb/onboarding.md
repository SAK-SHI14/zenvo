# Zenvo Clinic Onboarding

Getting started with Zenvo is designed to be simple for clinic reception staff.

## Step 1: Account & Clinic Setup (5 Minutes)
- Sign up with your clinic phone number and email.
- Add your clinic name, address, timings, and doctor profiles.

## Step 2: Connect Patient Appointments (10 Minutes)
- Connect Google Calendar, link your clinic HMS via API/webhook, or upload your daily appointment sheet (CSV/Excel).

## Step 3: Choose Reminder Templates (5 Minutes)
- Pick pre-approved WhatsApp, SMS, and voice reminder templates in English, Hindi, or Hinglish.
- Set default trigger times (recommended: 24 hours before and 2 hours before the appointment).

## Step 4: Test & Go Live (10 Minutes)
- Send a test reminder to your reception phone number.
- Switch on live reminders and monitor confirmations from the Zenvo dashboard.

## Guided Onboarding
- Pro and Enterprise customers receive a complimentary 30-minute live onboarding call with a Zenvo specialist.
