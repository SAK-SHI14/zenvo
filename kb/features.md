# Zenvo Features

Zenvo is an automated clinic appointment-reminder SaaS built to reduce patient no-shows by up to 60%.

## Core Capabilities
- **Multi-Channel Reminders**: Send scheduled reminders via WhatsApp, SMS, and automated voice calls 24 hours and 2 hours before each visit.
- **Two-Way Confirmation**: Patients can reply 1 to confirm, 2 to reschedule, or 3 to cancel without calling the front desk.
- **Multilingual Support**: Supports English, Hindi, and Hinglish messages and voice prompts.
- **Smart Calendar Sync**: Works standalone via CSV upload or syncs with Google Calendar and popular clinic management systems.
- **No-Show Analytics**: Daily dashboard showing confirmed visits, pending confirmations, and recovered appointments.
- **Post-Visit Follow-Ups**: Optional automated reminders for lab test collection and follow-up checkups.
