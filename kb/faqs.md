# Zenvo Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q: How quickly can our clinic go live with Zenvo?**
A: Most single-location clinics go live within 30 minutes using our self-serve onboarding or a 15-minute guided setup call.

**Q: Do patients need to download an app?**
A: No. Patients receive reminders directly on WhatsApp, standard SMS, or as a normal phone call.

**Q: What happens if we exceed our monthly reminder limit?**
A: Additional reminders are billed at Rs 1 per SMS/WhatsApp message on Basic and Pro plans, or you can upgrade anytime.

**Q: Can we customize the reminder timing and clinic name?**
A: Yes. You can set custom intervals (such as 24 hours and 2 hours before) and include your clinic name, doctor name, and Google Maps link.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes, Zenvo offers a 14-day free trial with up to 50 free reminders, no credit card required.

**Q: Does Zenvo integrate with our existing clinic software?**
A: Pro and Enterprise plans support webhook and API integrations, plus native sync with Google Calendar and major Indian clinic HMS tools.
