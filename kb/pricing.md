# Zenvo Pricing Plans

Zenvo offers three simple monthly plans for clinics and healthcare practices in India.

## Basic Plan - Rs 999 / month
- Up to 500 appointment reminders per month
- Automated SMS and WhatsApp reminders
- 1 clinic location and up to 2 doctor calendars
- Email support during business hours

## Pro Plan - Rs 2,499 / month
- Up to 2,500 appointment reminders per month
- Automated SMS, WhatsApp, and AI voice call reminders
- Up to 3 clinic locations and 10 doctor calendars
- Two-way patient confirmation and rescheduling links
- Priority phone and WhatsApp support

## Enterprise Plan - Custom Pricing
- Unlimited appointment reminders across multi-city clinic chains
- Dedicated account manager and custom EHR/HMS integration
- Custom sender ID and branded caller experience
- 99.9% uptime SLA and priority 24/7 support

## Billing Notes
- All prices exclude 18% GST.
- Annual billing gives 2 months free on Basic and Pro plans.
- No setup fee for Basic and Pro plans.
